# Yubi (fka CredAvenue)

> Yubi (formerly CredAvenue) is a Chennai-based Indian fintech operating a unified technology platform for debt and credit markets — connecting borrowers, lenders, and investors. It exposes self-serve REST APIs (first publicised for the Yubi Co.lend co-lending workflow) that let banks and NBFCs configure loan products and integrate their systems. Developer documentation is published on a Theneo-hosted developer portal.

## Products
- [Yubi Co.lend (co-lending)](https://www.go-yubi.com/co-lending): bank–NBFC co-origination and co-lending platform with self-serve integration APIs.
- [Yubi Loans](https://www.go-yubi.com/): corporate and enterprise lending.
- [Yubi products overview](https://www.go-yubi.com/): Pools (securitisation), Invest (fixed income), Flow (supply-chain finance), Build (infra/real-estate financing).
